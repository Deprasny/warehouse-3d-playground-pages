HISTORICAL V2 CREDIT / MODEL RECORD (superseded dimensions/layout details)

Roles: Panoramic, Luxury New Generation, Compartment Suites, Priority, Imperial.
Original procedural geometry authored for the Depras warehouse-3d-playground
project. Construction, gear and rig helpers reuse the project's original SSNG
toolkit. No third-party train mesh, source photograph texture, scanned geometry
or UV atlas is included. Paint, ornaments and lettering are authored geometry
and vertex colors. Each family has high, medium and low detail levels.

These are photo-informed miniature exteriors, not certified engineering models.
Hidden elevations, metric fitting and some opening counts are inferred. Imperial
combines unit K1 0 13 05 paint evidence with an unidentified family side view;
its full opening rhythm and exact bogie assignment remain provisional.
Luxury NG targets operational K1 0 23 01: muted champagne/gold-olive door color
#a89c61, one plug entry per side (two rigs total), white KAI contour mark and
script Luxury on the opposite blank end panel. Ten main panes per side and
their 0.62m station shift are game-fit estimates; opposite-side placement is
inferred. The v1 blue test appearance is superseded. Its cause is unproved;
it is not asserted to be protective film.
