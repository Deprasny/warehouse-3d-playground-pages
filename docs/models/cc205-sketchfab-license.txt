Modifications: rescaled to the game's CC206-relative length (CC205 17.678 m vs CC206 15.849 m over couplers);
wheels grown to the shared 0.46 rim radius with the body lifted to meet them and running gear squeezed onto the
game gauge; material-merged source segmented into wheel, bogie and body pivots; plain drawheads added; photo
livery packed into one texture atlas and tiling/grunge maps averaged into vertex colours; three simplified LODs
(meshoptimizer) with rebuilt normals; Meshopt-packed GLB. The original livery is retained.
The downloaded source file is not redistributed and remains unchanged.
