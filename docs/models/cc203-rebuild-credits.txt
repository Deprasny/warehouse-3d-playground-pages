CC203 independent exterior rebuild v9 / game adapter
Authored for Depras, 18 October 2026.

New hand-authored locomotive geometry; no imported train vertices or generated AI mesh.
KAI livery and unit markings are retained for identification.
Three Meshopt LODs, restored rigid anchors and shared miniature material finish.
The game adapter retargets gauge and raises/re-anchors the two couplers.

V7 bogie visuals reuse the independently authored CC206 V15 truck builder,
adapted to CC203 wheel stations, gauge and radius. The supplied JIONS GLB
was only a local visual comparator; its geometry/textures are not included.

V9 deepens the shared cast web for CC203 and adds lightweight approximate
inner motor/gearcase/support masses. Default CC206 geometry is unchanged.
