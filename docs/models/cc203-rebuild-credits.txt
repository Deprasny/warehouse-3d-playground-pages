CC203 independent exterior rebuild v13 / game adapter
Updated for Depras, 20 October 2026.

New hand-authored locomotive geometry; no imported train vertices or generated AI mesh.
KAI livery and unit markings are retained for identification.
Three Meshopt LODs, restored rigid anchors and shared miniature material finish.
The game adapter retargets gauge and raises/re-anchors the two couplers.

V13 follows scalar body/cab/hood/glazing landmarks from the user's supplied
JIONS CC203 V8 model by JIONS 1080 HD:
https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/jions-cc-203-locomotive-v80-3d-model-c0fdd66b2c8547a694596d1ee9835f66
The supplied model is a local visual reference only. Its mesh, texture and UV
data are not included in these independently authored game assets.

V7 bogie visuals reuse the independently authored CC206 V15 truck builder,
adapted to CC203 wheel stations, gauge and radius. The supplied JIONS GLB
was only a local visual comparator; its geometry/textures are not included.

V9 deepens the shared cast web for CC203 and adds lightweight approximate
inner motor/gearcase/support masses. Default CC206 geometry is unchanged.

V11 promotes the reviewed original cab/hood rebuild: tapered upper shoulders,
planar three-pane side glazing, primary95 08 sash proportions, shorter nose
fillet and a more upright front profile. Front optical groups stay rigid and
night-light positions follow the exported lens centers. The local red-orange
body band is separate from KAI logo orange. V9 dense bogies and all wheel,
bogie and coupler contracts are retained. No new photo textures are embedded.
Photo/camera fits remain approximate, not factory-certified measurements.

V14 follows the user-selected CC201 miniature hood cross-section: wider and
lower skin, retaining the CC203 cabin, longitudinal joins, livery and dense
bogie rig. This is an art-direction adjustment, not a factory dimension claim.
Logo shapes and round rear optics remain rigid; light metadata follows them.

V15 lowers the upper cabin rigidly to align the roof with the selected hood
crown, shortening only the lower transition zone. Window proportions and
optical circles are preserved. Body-mounted end-bay equipment fills the ends.

V16 follows the user's miniature art direction: the accepted original CC206
bogie/wheel kit, gauge, radius, axle stations and pivots replace CC203-specific
running gear on all three LODs. Supports and end-bay equipment adapt to the body.
This is a shared game kit, not a claim that prototype locomotive trucks match.
No geometry or textures from the supplied JIONS comparison GLB are included.

Three small cosmetic hex heads at each visible inspection circle are parented
to the existing wheel anchors to make miniature wheel rotation readable.
The bearing housings remain bogie-owned. This adds 144 triangles per LOD,
using existing vertex colors/materials, with no new per-frame animation code.
